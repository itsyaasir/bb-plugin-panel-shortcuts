Keep the panel pages you use most within reach of an open conversation.
Choose your shortcuts once and they appear as compact icon buttons in every
thread header.

## What you get

- One-click access to Browser, Terminal, VS Code, and other discovered panel actions.
- Pin or unpin pages in Settings, with changes shared across open windows.
- Native panel icons and page-name tooltips on hover or keyboard focus.
- An icon-only row without horizontal scrolling.

## How it works

Open a thread's right panel and select New tab once to discover actions from
installed plugins. Fixed views such as Info and Diff are discovered when
their panel is visible. Choose which pages to pin in the plugin's settings.
Shortcuts activate BB's existing controls and preserve each action's native
behavior, including starting a new terminal.

## Requirements

Requires BB 0.47 or later and Plugin SDK 0.6.79 or later. Browser requires BB
Desktop; VS Code and other plugin pages require their respective plugins.
The adapter follows BB's app-shell controls and may need updating after BB UI
changes. No external service or separate account is required by this plugin.
